# V5 sketch — Revision 1 (2026-08-20)

A sketch, not a spec. The V5 spec is written only after this document's
checklist is complete, with the V1–V4R discipline (hypotheses, falsifiers,
frozen intervals, sealed seeds). Reviews 31–35 and 38–41 are
incorporated; H19–H24 in PREDICTIONS.md (committed 2026-08-20, before
any V5 world) are the scaffolding.

Unlike the V4 sketch, this is not written into a vacuum. V4R's sealed
block has closed 7/7. V5.0 (the component rate-distortion frontier) has
already run. H19's causal grid is in flight on worlds 500–509 and is
NOT YET SCORED; nothing in this sketch may treat that grid as a result.
The hypotheses were frozen before the worlds existed. The protocol
deviation from the letter of H19 (rank at the generator, rather than
bits/scalar at the coder) is recorded below rather than silently
adopted.

## The V5 question, stated so it cannot drift

V4R asked when a library needs a lifecycle, and answered: not at this
scale, except for retention, which is birth run forwards. V5 asks:

    Under what conditions does the cheapest useful representation of
    accumulated computation stop being a static library of independently
    compressed abstractions, and can one prospective scoring rule track
    that boundary as code cost, reuse value, meta-recurrence, and
    horizon change?

One-line contrast: V4R is a census of which edits pay; V5 is a test of
whether the amortization law is quantitative, whether a second
representation class exists above compressed atoms, and whether a
single score can choose among classes. V3 is a maternity ward; V4R is
an audit of the household budget; V5 is whether the household can
allocate.

## Why V5 is not just "V4R plus more operations"

Four qualitatively new pressures enter, none of which a census exposes:

1. CAUSAL D, NOT A RE-SCORE. V4R confirmed RETAIN iff H_R * s_bar >
   lambda * D(A) at one value of D(A). Repricing D at the V5.0 frontier
   moves the crossing by arithmetic (carry / s_bar) and is not a test.
   V5 manipulates D and s independently at the generator and predicts
   the threshold *before* scoring.

2. A SECOND REPRESENTATION CLASS. V4R's FACTORIZE cell lost to matched-
   budget COMPRESS at every reachable library size. Without a class
   other than compressed atoms, "edit selection" is a forced choice.
   V5.2 has to *create* the missing cell with controlled meta-recurrence
   and support-per-family, or register that the 216-program world
   cannot instantiate one.

3. SEQUENTIAL VALUE. In an evolving library the carry cost of A is
   endogenous: deleting A can trigger replacement promotion A' and
   save 0 bits. Per-object rules are then ill-posed. V5.1 stays on the
   frozen-library protocol where D_retain - D_delete = D(A) exactly;
   V5.5 is where endogeneity is the phenomenon.

4. SELECTION, NOT DEMONSTRATION. V1–V4R demonstrated individual
   representations under experimenter-chosen regimes. V5.4 asks whether
   one prospective score chooses the right class when the regime is
   not announced. That is a different claim, and it is gated on (2).

## The nested claims V5 is really about

Three claims, nested. A later rung is not a consolation prize for an
earlier failure, and an earlier pass with a later block is a result.

    LAW            thresholds move as C/s says          (H19)
    PHASE DIAGRAM  a second representation class exists
                   at reachable (M, r_meta)             (H20, H21)
    SELECTION      one prospective score picks the
                   right class across regimes           (H22–H24)

If the law fails, stop quoting N*s > C as more than one fitted
crossing. If the law holds and the phase diagram never appears, the
library is a set of compressed atoms, not a language, and V6 macros
have no substrate — a respectable outcome, registered in advance. If
both hold and selection fails, we can demonstrate edits but not an
economy.

## Currency and resource model

Through V4R the gating instrument was 8 bits/scalar. V5.0 retired that
as a *gating* currency. It remains a continuity report.

    D_8bit(R)     8 bits per retained scalar (V1–V4R, comparable)
    D*(R; eps)    min bits/scalar s.t. Delta L_c <= eps, component c
                  scored only on computations that depend on it
    J             L_preq + lambda * D_T + kappa * SUM_t D_live(t)
                  + gamma * C_retrieval + mu * C_edit

lambda = ln 2, unchanged. kappa, gamma, mu stay at 0 with logging in
V5.1–V5.3, as in V4.1: occupancy and edit cost are not the H19
question. Any V5.4 claim that depends on a nonzero kappa/gamma/mu must
say so, and kappa = 0 remains a registered null.

New constitutional instrument rule:

    Compare representation classes after each has been locally
    compressed to its own rate-distortion frontier. A sharing claim
    at full precision against a compressed private alternative is
    the error that made V4.2 look positive.

Non-vacuity is a refuse-to-score, not a warning. A component with an
empty participant set is unscoreable at every depth.

H19's D(A) manipulation is at the GENERATOR (residual rank: 99 / 198 /
396 scalars at rank 1 / 2 / 4). That is the cut that cannot be an
accounting identity. Bits/scalar at the coder (the letter of the
PREDICTIONS.md entry) is a second arm, not a substitute: it still
moves carry by construction unless s_bar moves too. Both are reported.
The in-flight scorer currently prices carry as LN2 * 8 * scalars(rank);
the spec must dual-report proxy-bit D and frontier D* of the same
artifacts, and H19's ratio test is against whichever D was manipulated.

## Core hypotheses (H19–H24; already in PREDICTIONS.md)

Numbering is not provisional. Falsifiers and operational cuts below
are what this sketch adds.

### H19 — Code-cost invariance (V5.1; flagship of the LAW claim)

Prediction. Empirical retention threshold follows H*(D, s) =
lambda * D / s_bar across at least two independent manipulations of D
and two of s. No fitted threshold. Dual reading, both registered:

    (a) H* ∝ D at measured s_bar
    (b) H* · s_bar ∝ D even if s_bar moves with D

(b) is the law. (a) fails with a diagnosis if coarser operators are
less useful per reuse — the risk named in the original prediction,
not a post-hoc excuse.

Protocol. Frozen library at the gap so D_retain - D_delete = D(A)
exactly; zero post-gap births or the world is excluded; score the
RETURN WINDOW; count returning tasks before reading any number.
Primary D-arm: residual rank {1, 2, 4} (in flight on worlds 500–509,
horizons 48/56/64/72, gap (32, 40)). Required s-arm: same rank,
different per-use value (frequency-versus-value, or family strength);
without it inverse-proportionality is untested. Optional coder-arm:
store at 2/3/4/6/8 bits/scalar at matched rank, to honour the letter
of the frozen prediction as a sensitivity.

Falsifier. Observed H*-ratio vs D-ratio relative error >= 0.25 on (b)
after s_bar is measured, on both a D-manipulation and an s-
manipulation. A D-only pass with no s-arm is a partial, not a pass.
Re-scoring V4R O4 at D* is not a confirmation of H19.

Respectable partial. Rank ratios track (b) and the s-arm is noisy:
report (b) as supported for code cost, inverse-s as unconfirmed.

### H20 — Higher-order amortization (V5.2; flagship of the PHASE DIAGRAM)

Prediction. A COMPRESS-to-FACTORIZE crossing exists, and M*(r_meta) is
monotone non-increasing in r_meta. M small => COMPRESS even at decent
r_meta; M up and r_meta up eventually produce FACTORIZE.

Coordinates, not N. Family count F and support per family m, with
N ~= N_base + F*m. Holding m fixed isolates scale; holding F fixed
isolates evidence per abstraction. Raising F at fixed N starves every
candidate and shrinks the library — the confound that made the first
F-sweep invalid. Independently, r_meta in A_f(z) = C(z) + r_meta *
B(z; alpha_f) + sqrt(1-r_meta^2) * eps_f(z). Measured abstraction-
level recurrence must track configured r_meta before any FACTORIZE
oracle runs, analogue of V1's residual-function correlation.

Ceiling, registered. The canonical world caps tasks at 6**3 = 216
programs. N=256 is unreachable without adding primitives, which
breaks comparability. If no FACTORIZE cell exists at max reachable M
even at r_meta = 1, that is a TESTBED result, not an H20 failure to
be tuned away. A new generator with more primitives is a separately
fingerprinted world family, not a silent WorldConfig field.

Gates, in order, all mandatory:

    G1  existence: R^2_observed - R^2_isotropic_null > 0 at rank 2
    G2  economics: J_factorize < J_independent_compress at matched bits
        (not merely < J_full_precision_atoms)
    G3  leftover: D(E_i) << D(A_i) AND substantial shared capture
        (a fat E_i makes the family decorative)

Falsifier. No G2 win on the (F, m, r_meta) grid the testbed can reach,
including r_meta = 1 at maximum M with m held at V3's promotion
minimum. Then H20 is blocked by the world, the phase diagram does not
appear at ROW scale, and H22–H24 that depend on FACTORIZE as a live
class are blocked rather than failed.

### H21 — Prospective schema reuse (V5.3)

Prediction. A family A(z; alpha) fit on held-in members makes a novel
member cheaper to acquire by learning only alpha_new, versus a
complete new operator, on at least prequential cost and retained bits,
always against a matched-budget independently compressed operator.

Instrument. Leave-one-abstraction-out: fit C, B on A_1..A_{m-1}, infer
only alpha_m on proposal probes for held-out A_m. Compare learn-full /
independent-compressed / family / family+E / global-only. V4 recovered
~7.5% of the centre-only deficit with the current rank-2 family; H21
is not a rewrite of that diagnostic, it is the same instrument at the
r_meta that G1–G2 required.

Falsifier. Family acquisition does not beat matched-budget independent
compression on prequential cost AND retained bits. A win on bits alone
is storage; a win on loss at extra bits is capacity.

Gated on H20 G1. If no family structure exists, H21 has nothing to
acquire.

### H22 — Economic edit selection (V5.4)

Prediction. One prospective scoring rule (initially hand-designed, not
learned) matches the oracle-optimal edit in a majority of (regime,
world) cells, where different edits are oracle-optimal in different
registered regimes.

    low r              KEEP
    bloated atoms      COMPRESS
    repeated innovation  PROMOTE
    related atoms      FACTORIZE     (only if H20 G2 paid)
    future return      RETAIN
    obsolete           RETIRE        (only if a world instantiates it)

Gate, load-bearing. H22 is blocked, not failed, unless at least two
structurally different edits are each oracle-optimal in some
registered regime. A selector that always picks COMPRESS in a market
where only COMPRESS pays is unfalsifiable. Refusal cells are first-
class: the rule must NOT pick FACTORIZE where COMPRESS wins.

Oracle vs estimator are separate rungs. H22-oracle: Delta J computed
with access the learner will not have (future stream, true dependents).
Tests whether one *objective* selects. H22-online: Delta J estimated
from history only. Tests whether the learner can estimate it. Do not
collapse them. Teacher structure never enters the online estimator.

Falsifier. Accuracy at or below the majority-class baseline (always
pick the globally most common oracle edit) on cells where at least two
classes appear.

### H23 — Structural planning (V5.5)

Prediction. Under library evolution or nonstationarity, finite-horizon
rollout structural regret < myopic structural regret. In stationary
worlds with frozen libraries, myopic ~= oracle.

Protocol. Arms share history up to t, then diverge. Score the remaining
stream, never end-of-lifetime J after two libraries have evolved
apart. The clairvoyant oracle is a trajectory pi* = argmin_pi
J(future structural path), not a per-object table. Deleting A at task
32 is not a paired comparison once it changes what gets promoted next.

Falsifier. Rollout does not reduce regret relative to myopic wherever
edits change later promotions, or myopic is already at oracle in those
worlds (then path-dependence does not bind at this scale).

Gated on a world where the open-library carry is actually endogenous.
If every deletion saves D(A) because nothing is re-promoted, H23 is
unmotivated rather than false — same pattern as V4.1's empty DELETE.

### H24 — Learned restructuring (V5.6)

Prediction. A policy trained on (L_t, H_t, e, Delta J, outcome) tuples
has lower structural regret than the hand-designed estimator on held-
out *economic conditions* (held-out code cost, horizon, r_meta, M),
not merely held-out seeds of the same economy.

Falsifier. No improvement on held-out conditions, or improvement only
on held-out seeds of the training grid.

Gated on H22 producing a non-vacuous market and H23 producing a regret
signal. Training and evaluating on worlds 0–9 of one regime is not
this claim.

## Worlds (draft)

H19 retention world. Single-family, family onset early enough that
pre-gap abstractions form, gap (32, 40) or equivalent with a counted
return window, library frozen at the gap, retained vs deleted arms.
Validity: returning-task count >= minimum_cluster; zero post-gap
births; arms byte-identical to the gap. Rank and horizon vary; s-arm
is a different family-value or frequency schedule at matched rank.

H20 meta-recurrence world. NEW generator family, separately
fingerprinted. r_meta knob with a measured-recurrence diagnostic that
must pass before oracles. (F, m) grid with m held at or above
minimum_cluster. Structureless control: r_meta = 0 must reproduce
independent atoms (COMPRESS wins). Validity: measured r_meta tracks
configured; M grows with F at fixed m over some range, otherwise
scale is not instantiated.

H21 uses the H20 world at a point where G1 passed. Held-out member
never used to fit C, B.

H22 regime battery. Existing V3 structured / control / dormancy worlds
plus H20 cells, labelled with their oracle-optimal edit *before* the
selector runs. No new operator.

H23 open-library retention. Re-promotion restored, so carry is
endogenous. Validity: at least some deletions trigger a replacement
promotion (carry ~ 0) and some do not. If that split cannot be
produced, H23 waits.

Stationary frozen-library cells are the H23 null: myopic ~= oracle.

Every world carries a validity gate that is allowed to fail. A gate
that cannot fail is not a gate. Redesign the world before tuning.

## The learner, and what is not a learner yet

V5.1–V5.3 are oracles on frozen or controlled libraries. The V3
promoter stays frozen. No online FACTORIZE, no online selector, no
learned pi. This is the V4R census discipline applied to the new
knobs.

V5.4 introduces a deterministic greedy edit policy (generate
candidates, estimate Delta J, apply the best, recompute, repeat until
no edit improves — V4 spec §3.3, so edit order is not a hidden
hyperparameter). Hand-designed estimators from V1–V4R: H_R * s_hat vs
lambda D*(A) for RETAIN; matched-budget RD for COMPRESS vs FACTORIZE;
V3's promotion criterion unchanged and FROZEN (a learner that knows it
can FACTORIZE later may promote more freely; that interaction is an
open question, not a silent coupling).

V5.5 adds short-horizon rollout over that policy. V5.6 trains q_phi or
Q_hat on the decision dataset. Neither exists until the gates above
pass.

Never edit PromotingSharedResidualLearner. Subclass. The same will
apply to V5's own classes once a confirmation plan is hashed.

## Registered diagnostics and failure branches

Adopted before any remaining V5 run, so no failure is diagnosed with
an improvised instrument.

If H19 ratios do not track D: report s_bar by rank. If s_bar falls
with rank in proportion to D, (a) fails and (b) may still hold. If
s_bar is flat and H* is flat, the law is an artifact of one operating
point. If post-gap births exclude most worlds, the freeze failed and
the cell is unscoreable (do not impute).

If H20 G1 passes and G2 fails: geometry without economics, the V4.2
result one level up. Do not lower the matched-budget bar. If G2
passes only because E_i restores capacity, G3 fails and the family is
decorative.

If H20 never instantiates M large enough: report the ceiling and stop.
Do not add primitives inside the canonical fingerprint.

If H22 accuracy equals always-COMPRESS: the market was one-class; the
rung was blocked and should not have been scored as a fail or a pass.

If H23 myopic already matches oracle under open-library evolution:
path-dependence does not bind at this scale; sequential decision-
making is a true statement that this world does not exercise.

If the composition probe explodes at depth 8: V6–V8 are blocked; the
probe is a V5 addendum result and a publishable negative. Do not
infer composition from H19–H21.

## Branch table: what H19 and H20 do to the rest of this sketch

H19 is in flight. This table is the spec's section 0 once scored.

- H19 (b) holds on D-arm only: proceed, with inverse-s as unconfirmed;
  do not quote "inversely proportional to s" in the paper.
- H19 fails both readings: LAW claim dies; PHASE DIAGRAM may still be

- H20 G1 fails (no family geometry even at r_meta = 1): the r_meta
  generator is broken or neural abstractions do not share a

  Recursive-language story is in trouble at the representation

  V6. A macro must beat COMPRESS and every simpler counterfactual at

  matched-budget lesson applied to branching factor.

  the first point that would evidence the grand thesis. V5 does not

- Interactive worlds, dreaming, experimentation: V10. Hard line.
  Existing lifetime infrastructure does not transfer.
- Reasoning macros, procedural games, ARC: V11+. ARC is an exam, not

Also deferred, unchanged from V3 §8 / V4 §7: equivalence-class routes,
hidden-basis coordinate discovery, memory hierarchy, task-boundary

Search/synthesis cost may be LOGGED in V5 (candidate counts, fit

## Open questions this sketch does NOT settle

Recorded so they are not mistaken for oversights, and so a later
choice cannot be presented as having been planned.

1. Known remaining horizon vs estimated P(return). H19 can give the
   learner H_R; H22-online maybe cannot. The estimator is not frozen.
2. Whether PROMOTE's threshold stays frozen while V5 edits run. V4
   froze it. The interaction (promote more freely if you can
   compress/factorize/delete later) is real and unmeasured.
3. Whether kappa, gamma, mu are ever swept in V5 or stay zero-with-
   logging. Charging edit cost changes H22's market.
4. Whether H20 is allowed a new primitive count (new world family) or
   must stay inside 6 x 3 = 216. This sketch permits a separately
   fingerprinted family and forbids a silent config change; it does
   not require the new family.
5. Whether H22-oracle may use teacher structure. This sketch says no
   except post-hoc scoring. If development shows the hand-designed
   rule needs an oracle factorization bound, that is a new
   instrument, declared before use.
6. What "majority of (regime, world) cells" means when cells share a
   world. The spec must freeze a clustering (world-level first, as
   V1 did for initializations).
7. Whether the composition probe is a V5 checklist item or only a V6
   entry criterion. This sketch puts it on the V5 checklist so V6 is
   not started on a broken foundation; it is not a V5 hypothesis.
8. How to treat 500–509: development only, never confirmatory. If
   H19's first score is taken from them, that score is a development
   result, like worlds 0–9 for V1.
9. Rank vs bits as the "official" D manipulation. This sketch makes
   rank primary and bits/scalar a sensitivity. The spec may reverse
   that if the s-confound on rank is fatal and the coder-arm is
   cleaner; it may not treat them as interchangeable after seeing
   numbers.
10. The unit of forgetting / old-task interference, still unresolved
    from V4 §11, and not V5.1's problem.

## Claims not made

No claim of relevance to ARC-AGI. No claim that FACTORIZE, MERGE,

is the test of whether they can be made to. No claim that a learned
policy is required for V5 to count as a version. No claim that D* is

upper bound on true description length, conservative for both arms).
No claim that V5.0's shared-cheaper-than-private generalises beyond
this coder and this family. No n=3 bootstrap as an inferential claim.
