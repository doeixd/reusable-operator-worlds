# Interaction-net / graph-rewrite substrate — design sketch (NOT a plan)

Status: SKETCH ONLY. Nothing here is frozen, registered, or licensed. No
experiment is proposed and no compute will be spent on it. Staging authority
lives in `POST_E6_RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md` Tracks C/D, `RF_ROLE_FILLER_PLAN.md`
RF3/RF4, and the cited reviews — this note is a dependency map and reading
guide, and it retires the day a rung's gates are met and a real plan freezes.

## 1. The idea, as registered across four reviews

The stack (review 84's box):

    role-filler language -> typed graph IR -> local rewrite execution

- Review 80: do NOT build it to prove "programs are possible" — that is
  banked (E1/E2/E8). Test it on what the flat pipeline does not naturally
  expose: branching, recursion, loops, macros, structural rewriting,
  language growth. Arc: synthesize -> invent primitives -> typed
  graph/rewrite -> branching -> iteration/recursion -> macro extraction ->
  refactoring -> learned search over a self-improving language.
- Review 81: longer roadmap ends in graph-rewrite/editable IR, then
  amortized synthesis revisited ONLY when search becomes expensive
  (branching expands search; loops add latent control; programs lengthen).
  Key update: the learned language makes direct optimization cheap, so a
  writer is currently irrational — do not ask new vocabulary to rescue
  unbroken search.
- Review 82: entry condition is macro invention SUCCEEDING; the payoff is
  abstractions over branches, repeats, loops, reusable subgraphs. Sharpened
  hypothesis: abstraction helps synthesis by reducing EFFECTIVE PROGRAM
  LENGTH, not nominal search-space cardinality.
- Review 84: raw ports are probably not the learned representation;
  semantic roles are (IF = {condition, true, false}); the compiler layer
  maps roles onto ports internally. Formation split: continuous neural
  learning -> factorized role/filler representation -> discrete program
  manipulation ("learning mechanism != representational structure").
- E6 plans (E5/E6/E6.2): all three explicitly defer it — E5 until search
  is expensive, E6.2 until after macro invention, E6 until the question is
  subgraphs rather than contiguous subsequences.

    for SO2, which waits for that line. Nothing about IF/REPEAT is

G3. Role-filler as more than description (Track A). RF0a: raw local
    semantics survive (means 0.764/0.661), role adds ~0. RF0b UNRESOLVED
    (heterogeneous rebound; null failure in the strongest cell).
    Label-free canonicalizer NOT opened; C1 waits for RF1.
G4. Opportunity gates on the exact artifact (Track C's standing rule, from
    the iteration/branch episodes). Fixed straight-line approximability
    must be re-measured wherever IF/REPEAT is claimed — the two-minute
    teacher-side gates that killed fixed-length iteration (contraction)
    and branching (weak operators). No learner trains before G4 passes.

## 4. The economic motive is conditional, not missing

Review 81 locates when this substrate pays: programs structurally richer,
branching expanding search, loops adding latent control, thousands of
queries. E5.1 measured C_find linear in length / logarithmic in space —
cheap NOW. So the motive arrives with C2-class worlds, and any proposal
must name which cost (C_find, description, or both per E6's H-law
`H s_desc + beta H s_search > D*(A)`) the graph structure reduces, with
OPT-as-teacher distillation (review 81) as the writer baseline rather
than learning from scratch against a single exact route (E0/E3 gauge
warning: symbolic identity is underdetermined).

## 6. What may be done before the gates (no lifetimes, no learned runs)

  substrate or generator.
- Censuses over cached routes with the alphabet tax charged (loop-census
  pattern) — remembering the category discipline: amortization censuses
  do NOT retire constitutive constructs (macros/loops/branches are what
  "program" means); a negative redirects to generator design, never to
  dropping the construct.
- Design writing (this note). No frozen plan for any rung until its Track
  C/D/RF gates are recorded as passed in PROGRESS.md.

## 7. Open questions (not tasks)

- Which substrate is both control-flow-identifiable and learnable?
  Canonical = learnable but unnecessary; rotated = necessary but
  unacquired. The conjunction is unlocated; SO1 may bound it via D_form.
- Supplied or discovered roles? RF4 decides whether roles are learned
  before any IR uses them (review 84's cleaner mapping depends on it).
- Does invented vocabulary shorten effective length (review 82) anywhere
  above linear subsequences? The E6 margin (~3-7x crossing) is the number
  to beat, on non-degenerate structure.
