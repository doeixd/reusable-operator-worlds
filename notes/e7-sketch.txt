E7 SKETCH -- parameterized macros, and the inference problem E5 did not test
Written 2026-08-29, after the E6 line closed. NOT a plan and NOT registered;
a plan must be frozen before any code, as always.

================================================================================
1. THE CRITIQUE E6 MAKES OF ITSELF
================================================================================

H39's confirmed central lesson is that the useful abstraction is

    A(alpha) + eps

-- parameterized, made during learning, with cheap coordinates for novelty --
and NOT a rigid `A`. That was established on tensors, sealed on seeds 700-729.

The entire E6 line then tested `A`. A macro is a CONSTANT fragment: `M := A B C`
is a fixed symbol sequence with no argument. Read in that light, E6's results
stop looking like facts about macros and start looking like a rediscovery, in
linguistic currency, of what H39 already established about tensors:

    E6A  a rigid macro pays in description, but only ~3-7x its crossing
    E6D  its creation cannot be timed from a retrospective code
    E6.2 it cannot be compiled at matched budget at all

We should not have expected otherwise. The object under test was the one the
project's own confirmed result says is the wrong object.

================================================================================
2. THE NUMBER THAT MAKES THIS CONCRETE
================================================================================

E6 step 0 measured MOTIF SURVIVAL: the fraction of planted recurrences that
produce the SAME learner gram.

    world 0   7/32 = 22%
    world 1  14/32 = 44%
    world 2  13/32 = 41%
    mean            ~36%

We treated the missing ~64% as gauge noise that limited the rung. That reading is
available but it is not the only one. Those are recurrences of the SAME
COMPUTATION written as DIFFERENT SYMBOL SEQUENCES -- which is exactly what a
parameterized macro should capture and a rigid one structurally cannot.

    A rigid macro collects only exact repeats.
    A parameterized macro `M(alpha)` should collect the family.

QUANTITATIVE PREDICTION, to be registered before it is measured: a parameterized
macro's effective use count is ~2-3x a rigid one's on the SAME corpus, because it
collects the near-misses. E6A's realized 22-56 uses against a crossing of 7.44
becomes a materially different economic picture at 60-150.

It also attacks E6.2's capacity obstruction from the right side. A single
composition needed ~4x a library slot to represent. But a FAMILY of related
compositions may share a low-dimensional argument even when no individual member
is representable at matched budget -- the family can be cheaper per member than
any one member is alone.

================================================================================
3. THE FREE PRE-CHECK, WHICH DECIDES WHETHER TO OPEN THE LINE AT ALL
================================================================================

Before building anything: measure, on the EXISTING E6 corpora, whether the ~64%
of non-matching recurrences form a LOW-DIMENSIONAL FAMILY in function space.

    - take the planted sites whose learner gram is NOT the modal image
    - compute each one's effective task-conditioned operator on a common probe
      (E5's lesson: two functions can only be compared at the SAME inputs)
    - ask whether those operators lie near a low-dimensional subspace, scored
      LEAVE-ONE-OUT (V5's lesson: never fit and score on the same objects)

This is arithmetic over cached routes. No new compute, no lifetimes.

    structured   -> `M(alpha)` has a real economic case; open the rung
    unstructured -> the argument channel buys nothing, `M(alpha)` collapses to
                    `M`, and the line is closed before it is built

This is the opportunity gate applied to a language question: measure that the
opportunity EXISTS before building the machine to exploit it. It is the same
discipline that should have preceded two dormancy designs and the B2 gate.

================================================================================
4. THE INFERENCE PROBLEM E5 DID NOT TEST
================================================================================

The thesis says adaptation is IDENTIFY-PRIMITIVE + INFER-ARGUMENT + SMALL PATCH.

E5 asked for `q(p | D)` -- write the whole program -- and it failed on quality
while search stayed cheap. But that is not the decomposition the thesis predicts.
The narrower object is

    q(alpha | D, p)

given the program's structure, infer its argument. That is a far smaller
inference problem, continuous rather than combinatorial, and it is the half the
H39 line actually confirmed works. E5's negative does not touch it.

If amortization pays anywhere in this substrate it should be here. And if it does
NOT, that is a much more damaging result for the thesis than E5 was -- which is
precisely why it is worth running. E5 tested a decomposition the thesis never
claimed; this tests the one it does.

Note the asymmetry to register in advance: `q(alpha | D, p)` is given the
program. That is a real information advantage over E5's writer and must be
disclosed as such -- the comparison to E5 is NOT apples to apples, and the rung
should carry a control where `p` is given to the E5-style writer too.

================================================================================
5. TWO CHEAPER DIAGNOSTICS, BOTH FROM E6.2's LEFTOVERS
================================================================================

CLOSURE. E6.2 found that one operator at matched budget cannot represent three
composed, with a converged fit -- so the function class is NOT CLOSED UNDER
COMPOSITION at fixed capacity. The vocabulary can EXPRESS compositions it cannot
INTERNALIZE. That is a structural bound on what primitive invention could mean
here, and it does not depend on the coding scheme.

There is a hint in the data: world 1 was the ONLY world that passed at matched
budget, and its macro `(8,8,5)` contains a REPEATED operator. Test: does the
effective rank / spectral profile of `P_c o P_b o P_a` predict matched-budget
compilability across many fragments? If it does, we get a FUNCTIONAL criterion
for which fragments are worth compiling -- a property of the function rather than
of the accounting. Hours, no lifetimes. Feeds directly into section 3.

ATTRACTOR COLLAPSE. At depth >= 8 all three worlds converge on a single
per-library slot (2, 11, 7) and the inferred program stops describing the
computation while the executor still solves the task. This was found incidentally
and labelled a scope limit. It deserves its own rung: it marks where the PROGRAM
abstraction fails even though the EXECUTOR does not, which bounds the whole
export-branch story, and nothing currently measures it directly.

================================================================================
6. A DEFLATIONARY READING THAT BELONGS IN THE PAPER
================================================================================

The standard motivation for library learning is that libraries make search
tractable. E5.1 killed that here: `C_find` is linear in program length and
logarithmic in program count, and route optimization matched or beat the oracle
in a 2.99M-program space. E6 then showed the description saving is small and the
creation timing undecidable from the past.

So in this substrate the value of the learned vocabulary is that it EXISTS and
TRANSFERS, not that it makes anything cheaper. That is the honest summary and it
should be stated rather than left implicit, because it is a real negative result
about WHY one would build a library at all.

================================================================================
7. ORDERING
================================================================================

    (a) the free pre-check of section 3          -- decides whether to proceed
    (b) the closure diagnostic of section 5      -- hours, feeds (a)'s reading
    (c) parameterized macros `M(alpha)`          -- the main line
    (d) `q(alpha | D, p)`                        -- the thesis's own adaptation claim
    (e) attractor collapse                       -- bounds the program story
    (f) interaction-net / graph-rewrite substrate -- review 82 places it after
        macro invention, tested on branching, loops and reusable subgraphs

(a) and (b) cost almost nothing and can reorder everything after them. Neither
should be skipped on the grounds that the direction already looks right.

================================================================================
8. METHODOLOGICAL, CARRIED FORWARD
================================================================================

Five estimands or controls in the E6 line turned out unable to fail, each caught
before compute: an algebraic crossing, a definitional substitutability test, two
arithmetic refusal controls, and a tolerance that a random operator passed. That
is past the point where a written rule is the right fix.

PROPOSAL: the estimand analogue of `src/row/arm_provenance.py`. Require every
registered estimand to ship with a NULL CONSTRUCTION under which it must return
the negative, and assert it as a test. One mechanism would have caught all five.
