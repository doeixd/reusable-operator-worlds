identifiability-sketch.txt
2026-09-22. Working sketch, NOT a plan and NOT preregistered. Written after
the depth-five gate closed the fourth L0d opportunity gate with no ambiguity
found. Its job is to name the mechanism behind a long run of negatives and to
propose the smallest generator change that could create the phenomenon the
program has been trying to measure. Everything here is conjecture until SG0
(SYNTHESIS_OPPORTUNITY_GATE_PLAN.md) measures the headroom it assumes.


ONE CAUSE, NOT EIGHT

Rungs that failed for absence of opportunity rather than learner failure:

  loop census 8-31   straight-line generator has no iteration to price
  E6.2               same-sized neural compilation is the wrong macro
  E7                 no real parameterized-macro family exists to find
  H47                learner rationally ignores true groups; the
                     told-membership oracle is WORSE on present cost
  H48b               identity has no present value to detect
  H49                discriminating signal exists only on a representation
                     already organized around the true structure
  E9                 construct unbuildable after five amendments
  L0d x4             no route ambiguity exists to measure

Two more that are usually read as learner failures but belong here:

  E5                 the writer lost to enumeration on COST and to the oracle
                     on QUALITY; search at 0.30 s/task against 7.6-36.7 s/task
                     of amortized training
  E5.1               space grew 3.58e7-fold, route-optimization seconds grew
                     3.30-fold, quality held at oracle parity

Conjecture: these are one fact. ROW scores regression with a smooth loss on
support and query examples drawn from the SAME distribution, with 128 examples
in d=16 against a composition of 3-5 operators chosen from 12 slots. The
support data massively OVERDETERMINES the route. Everything follows:

  - gradient route optimization matches an oracle, because the objective is
    smooth and the optimum is isolated;
  - search cost is logarithmic in program count, because length, not count, is
    the structural parameter;
  - an amortized proposer cannot pay, because the incumbent is already at the
    ceiling;
  - reducing support changes nothing, because even one example gives 16
    equations;
  - there is no route ambiguity, because there is nothing left underdetermined.

"Search is easy" and "there is no ambiguity" are the same statement seen from
E5 and from L0d. That is the sharpest thing the last year of negatives says,
and it is a property of the GENERATOR, not of any learner.


WHAT MAKES PROGRAM INDUCTION HARD, AND WHAT ROW IS MISSING

Real synthesis is hard because the examples do not pin the program. The
learner must supply a PRIOR over programs to choose among hypotheses that all
fit the evidence. That is precisely what an amortized proposer is for, and
precisely the situation ROW has never created.

ROW has instead been made hard along axes that do not produce this:

  program-space SIZE        E5.1 grew it 3.58e7-fold: no effect
  support SIZE              L0d cut it 128 -> 1: no effect
  operator STRENGTH         the rotated substrate: created an acquisition
                            problem (co-formation fails) without creating an
                            inference problem
  DEPTH                     depths 3-10 executed and searched fine

Size, evidence count, strength and depth are all the wrong knob. The missing
one is IDENTIFIABILITY.


THE SMALLEST CHANGE THAT COULD CREATE IT

Draw support and query inputs from DIFFERENT regions of the state space.

Today `train_x` and `eval_x` are independent draws from the same standard
normal, so agreeing on support implies agreeing on query. If support inputs are
restricted — to a low-dimensional subspace, a cone, a shell, a sign orthant —
then several routes can fit the support exactly and diverge on query. The
learner must then choose among evidence-equivalent programs, which is the
decision a prior exists to make.

Why this knob and not another:

  - it changes NO operator, library, depth, slot count or loss; only the input
    distribution of the support set, which keeps every existing artifact,
    evaluator and scorer usable and every comparison paired;
  - it is continuous and therefore sweepable, giving a measured coordinate
    (support-subspace dimension, or measured near-tie disagreement) rather than
    a configured one — the V1 lesson about measured versus configured knobs;
  - it makes search QUALITY-limited instead of COST-limited, which is the only
    regime where a learned prior can beat enumeration. Enumeration can still
    find every route that fits the support; it cannot say which one is right.
    That is a gap a proposer can close and a bigger search cannot.
  - it is the same mechanism as held-out generalization in real program
    induction, so a positive result would transfer rather than being an artifact
    of the knob.

Alternatives considered and ranked below it:

  target noise on support      creates uncertainty but not structured
                               ambiguity; near-ties would be random, so a
                               prior over programs has nothing to exploit
  operators agreeing on a      equivalent to the above in effect but requires
  subregion                    changing the library, breaking artifact reuse
  discrete/thresholded loss    would remove the smooth gradient that makes OPT
                               work, but also removes the paired continuous
                               estimands the whole program is built on; a much
                               larger change, and it attacks the SOLVER rather
                               than the PROBLEM
  fewer support examples       already tried and dead (L0d x2)


WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE GATED BEFORE ANY OF THIS IS A PLAN

This is a generator change, so the V5 Revision-3 rule binds: everything
downstream of the generator must be re-read, not only the definition edited.
At minimum:

  ACHIEVABILITY   the TRUE route must remain query-optimal. If restricting
                  support makes the true program no longer the best answer,
                  the world is broken, not ambiguous.
  NON-VACUITY     at least some routes must be evidence-equivalent on support
                  and materially different on query — measured, not assumed.
                  This is exactly SG0's near-tie disagreement statistic.
  BALANCE         per-task difficulty, operator-usage marginals and
                  per-program description length within 10% across the sweep,
                  or the knob is confounded (the V5.1 rule).
  NOT-TOO-STRONG  if the support determines nothing, every route ties and the
                  setting is uninterpretable rather than hard. Register the
                  upper end of the sweep against a measured floor.
  LINEARITY       report effects against MEASURED identifiability, not against
                  the configured subspace dimension.
  PAIRING         support restriction must be applied identically to every arm
                  in a comparison, and the query set must be untouched.

And the honest prior: this could fail the way r_ctrl is expected to fail — by
moving task difficulty along with ambiguity. That is what the balance gate is
for, and a failure there makes the sweep unscoreable rather than negative.


ORDER

SG0 first, on artifacts we already hold. It measures whether headroom exists
TODAY. Only a NO-HEADROOM or HEADROOM-LOCALIZED result makes this sketch worth
turning into a plan, and the two outcomes point at different plans:

  NO-HEADROOM            the substrate cannot host the phenomenon; this sketch
                         becomes the successor generator's opportunity census,
                         and the proposer branch on the current substrate is
                         closed rather than deferred.
  HEADROOM-LOCALIZED     headroom already exists somewhere; freeze the L0d
                         census around the statistic that localizes it, and
                         hold this sketch in reserve for widening the regime.
  HEADROOM-UNEXPLAINED   do not build on either; that is the route-margin
                         failure repeating.

Nothing here licenses opening a world band, and nothing here is a prediction
until it is registered inside a frozen plan.
