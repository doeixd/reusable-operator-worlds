# What G5R Stage D means — synthesis note (2026-09-07)

Status: INTERPRETATION, written after the verdict was recorded (PREDICTIONS.md
2026-09-05, commit 8b9b802). It adds no estimand and revises no verdict. Where
it goes beyond the registered ladder it says so.

# The question Track B is asking

Everything ROW has confirmed — reuse lowers lifetime cost (V1-V2), the
amortization law (V4R-V5), export and composition on sealed seeds 800-829 — was
on the ORDINARY substrate, whose operators are simple low-rank residuals.
Control flow (branching, iteration, the graph-rewrite stack in
`notes/interaction_net_sketch.txt`) needs a STRONGER substrate: operators with a
learned orthogonal map, the rotated family of `ROTATED_SUBSTRATE_SPEC.md`. G5R
asked whether a lifetime learner forms that stronger vocabulary the way it forms
the ordinary one. It failed 0/3, and Stages A-D have been asking why.

# Three facts, each isolated by a different cell

1. THE SUBSTRATE EXISTS. Given true routes and 262,144 example-gradients the
   learner recovers it on 2 of 3 worlds (Stage C / C_hi). This is not a
   representational impossibility.
2. A LIFETIME CANNOT AFFORD IT. With true routes handed over and every other
   difficulty removed — no sequence, no replay, no interference — the
   lifetime's own budget (~16,000 example-gradients) reaches median 0.79-0.90
   against 0.05 (C_lo, 0/3) and is still descending at its last checkpoint.
   The failure is ACQUISITION COST, not routing or forgetting. This retires
   the previous stage's route-inference/interference dichotomy.
3. EVEN WHEN BUDGET IS SOLVED, ROUTING IS NOT. At the budget where oracle
   routes succeed, learned routes fail on every world (L_hi 1.13-1.18) —
   confidently wrong rather than undecided, with a trajectory that WORSENS as
   the temperature anneal hardens the routes (1.7 at update 256, 2.0-2.2 at
   1,024, 1.13-1.18 at 4,096). Two walls, not one, and they are independent.

And a fourth, descriptive: with routes fixed, a task loses about half its fit
after its own training ends (O_or terminal-minus-end-of-task +0.50 to +0.53).
Replay is not holding the rotated operators in place. That is a third obstacle
waiting on the online path, unmeasured as a cause.

# What it means for the thesis

It sharpens rather than threatens it. The thesis has converged on "intelligence
MAKES its coordinate system while learning" (the H39 lesson: made, not mined).
Stage D says the cost of making a coordinate system scales steeply with how
much structure it carries: the ordinary vocabulary forms for free inside a
lifetime; the strong one costs at least 16x more gradient AND a route-discovery
capability the learner does not have. The gap between "a vocabulary exists" and
"a learner forms it unaided" is now a LOCATED, MEASURABLE frontier — and it is
the same frontier the formation line (H47-H53) reached from the other side,
where the structureless sham beat every structural hypothesis because the
objective never valued the structure.

# What it means practically

- Control flow stays closed. No IF/REPEAT work is licensed (C2 waits on SO2);
  the interaction-net sketch stays a sketch.
- SO1 becomes a quantitative question: HOW MUCH budget, and WHICH of update
  count / batch diversity / total example-gradients actually matters —
  bracketed by Stage D (16k-262k) rather than guessed. Its design must contain
  matched pairs that isolate at least two axes; `4096x64` vs `8192x2` moves all
  three.
- The most likely end state is Track B's second stop rule: "the substrate
  exists but the writer cannot acquire it." L_hi 0/3 is a registered prior for
  exactly that. If it lands, the rotated parameterization closes as not
  lifetime-learnable — an informative negative: ROW's learner can REUSE strong
  computation it is given, but cannot SELF-ORGANIZE it. The successor question
  is then about the acquisition MECHANISM (curriculum; teacher-assisted routes
  as a labelled crutch that can never become the non-oracle claim), not about
  more compute.
- The non-monotone L_hi trajectory is a mechanism HYPOTHESIS for the routing
  wall — soft mixtures fit, hardening commits to wrong routes — and belongs in
  a future ROUTE_INFERENCE-branch plan, not in SO1. It is not registered
  anywhere and must not be treated as a finding.

# What it does not mean

Not that the substrate is unlearnable in principle — only within this budget
and this parameterization. Not that the thesis is wrong. World 0 dissents
wherever the oracle is involved (C_hi 0.72, C_lo worst) and is the BEST world
in the learned-route and online cells, so "2 of 3" is doing real work in every
sentence above, and a world's difficulty is a property of a (world, arm) pair.
