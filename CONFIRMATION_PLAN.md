# V1 confirmation plan (frozen before opening sealed worlds)

Frozen at commit ed90ee2, 2026-08-17, before any seed in 100-129 was run or
inspected. No edits after the first confirmatory run starts; deviations, if
forced (e.g. crashed runs), are documented in PROGRESS.md and reported.

# Protocol

- Worlds: seeds 100-129 (30 worlds), generated by the canonical
  `configs/v1.yaml` world settings.
- Conditions: configured `rho` in [0.0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9, 1.0]; models
  Continuous and Dense-C with the frozen stage-two configurations exactly
  as in `configs/v1.yaml` at the frozen commit; forward task order;
  canonical replay (1:1); fast-tuning evaluation mode (no lifetime
  checkpoints, one novel task, no extended diagnostics), matching the
  development sweep protocol.
- Driver: `python -m row.experiments.sweep_rho --worlds 100..129
  --output artifacts/rho_confirmatory` (resumable; fingerprint-validated).
- 360 paired lifetimes total. Every run that completes is included; a run
  that crashes is retried once, and if it fails again the entire world is
  excluded for all conditions and models (world-level exclusion only) and
  reported.

# Pre-registered primary outcomes (one per claim; Holm-corrected family)

1. **Exact-reuse advantage.** At `rho = 1.0`: paired per-world
   Dense-C-minus-Continuous cumulative prequential Gaussian log loss.
   Claim confirmed if the mean is positive with two-sided exact sign-test
   p < 0.05 across 30 worlds.
2. **Recurrence dependence (the causal claim).** Slope of the per-world
   paired effect against measured functional recurrence (per-world linear
   regression of effect on measured recurrence across the six conditions;
   the 30 per-world slopes are the replicates). Confirmed if the mean
   slope is positive with two-sided sign-test p < 0.05.
3. **Sign reversal.** Continuous wins at `rho = 1.0` and loses at
   `rho = 0.0` within the same world. Confirmed if this joint pattern
   holds in a majority of worlds with two-sided sign-test p < 0.05
   against the 50% null.

# Pre-registered secondary outcomes (reported, not gated)

- Novel 32-shot NMSE paired effects at each `rho`.
- Crossover location per world, interpolated in both configured-`rho` and
  measured-recurrence coordinates; cross-world dispersion in each.
- Linearity: R^2 of the mean effect against measured recurrence vs
  configured rho (the development-stage 0.97-vs-0.65 contrast).
- Per-example normalization of all totals (nats per online example).
- Truncated-lifetime crossover rho*(N) at N in {16, 32, 64} tasks.

# Reporting rules

Inherited verbatim from RELEASE_PLAN.md and V2 spec section 5: development
and confirmatory numbers never mixed unlabeled; "crossover" not "phase
transition"; falsified or weakened claims reported as such; medians and
bootstrap percentile intervals at n = 30; the number-hygiene traps in V2
spec Appendix A checked against any text quoting these results.

# What confirmation does NOT cover

Shared-residual (Model 5), MDL gating (Model 4), hypernetwork, discrete,
oracle, robustness, and checkpoint results remain development-stage
findings and are labeled as such wherever reported. Only the three primary
outcomes above become confirmatory claims.
