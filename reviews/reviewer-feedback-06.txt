On worlds 3–9, with the corrected learnable-(\alpha) architecture, Continuous wins every paired exact-reuse comparison by **2,554–5,054 cumulative Gaussian-log-loss units**, mean about **3,787**, and improves mean 32-shot novel NMSE from **0.00731 to 0.00368**.
