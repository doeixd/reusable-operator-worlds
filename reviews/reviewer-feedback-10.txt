# Reviewer feedback 10 — anticipated reception, folded into the plans

Prompted by the PI's question: assuming V1 confirms and V2's hypotheses
hold, is this interesting, and what will others say? The full analysis is
now encoded in two places — V2 spec section 10 ("Anticipated objections
and positioning") and RELEASE_PLAN.md ("Write-up requirements") — so the
paper answers its reviewers before they ask. This entry records the
substance and the resulting changes.

## The forecast, in brief

- **Best audiences:** continual/meta-learning (a located, nuisance-
  invariant, mechanism-tested crossover where they currently have
  anecdotes) and MDL/compression (prequential code-length as a selection
  criterion; a compressor that refuses to compress noise).
- **Predictable objections:** "it's a toy" (answer: the toyness is the
  instrument; no scale claims); "it's circular" (answer: H6 — if the
  crossover shifts predictably under family mismatch, prior alignment
  becomes a parameter of the theory rather than a caveat); "neural
  DreamCoder" (answer: criterion, H8b refusal, measured frontier — cite
  generously); "we already knew this" (answer: located and predicted
  beats intuited and untested).
- **Meta-reception:** the agent-executed provenance will attract both
  interest and extra scrutiny; the audit trail exists precisely so the
  work can be verified without trusting its authors. One plain paragraph,
  no defensiveness.
- **Realistic ceiling, stated internally:** strong niche paper plus
  adoptable benchmark plus possibly the founding measurement of an
  "economics of abstraction" program. Not a capability demo. The most
  valuable single result if it holds is rho*(N) moving as predicted — a
  confirmed, falsifiable economic law of representation learning.

## Changes made

1. **V2 spec, H6:** given explicit "strategic weight" — co-primary with
   H5, because its outcome dictates how the central claim can be worded.
   The circularity objection is the paper's biggest exposure and H6 is
   the experiment that converts it into a coordinate of the theory.
2. **V2 spec, new section 10:** the seven-part objections-and-positioning
   section, including the claims-not-made list (10.5) to be printed
   verbatim in the paper, and the internal ceiling statement (10.7):
   optimize for unimpeachable, not impressive. Research statement moved
   to section 11.
3. **RELEASE_PLAN.md:** seven binding write-up requirements added,
   including the dedicated circularity section (written as if it were
   the abstract — skeptics read it first), the headline-selection rule
   (rho*(N) leads if confirmed), and the venue expectation.

## One standing instruction for the loop

When results arrive that bear on these objections — H6 especially — update
V2 spec section 10 in the same commit as the result. The objections
section must always reflect the current best answer, because it is the
part of the eventual paper that determines whether the rest is believed.
