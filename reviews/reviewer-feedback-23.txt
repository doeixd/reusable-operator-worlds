# One subtle issue I'd check: what distribution is (A) being fit on?

Even with fixed primitive position, the residual at that step may receive different incoming hidden states across tasks.

So there are two notions of “same function”:

### Common-domain functional similarity

Evaluate every residual on the same probe distribution:

[
z\sim Z_{\rm common}.
]

This asks whether the operators themselves are equivalent.

### On-trajectory substitutability

Evaluate on states actually arriving at that step:

[
z\sim Z_\tau^{\rm trajectory}.
]

This asks whether substituting the abstraction preserves the computation people actually care about.

I would measure both.

A candidate could look different globally but be perfectly substitutable on the relevant manifold.

For PROMOTE, **substitutability should ultimately win**.
