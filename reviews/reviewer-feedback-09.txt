# Reviewer feedback 09 — release decision

Short entry recording a PI-level decision, so the loop does not have to
infer it from conversation.

**Decision: no public sharing of results before V1 confirmation.** The
full rationale, the release gate checklist, the binding reporting rules,
and the repo-hygiene items now live in `RELEASE_PLAN.md` (top level).
Summary of the reasoning:

1. The development results are strong (unanimous crossover, acquired
   learning-to-learn 10/10 at 64 tasks, robustness invariance, the
   hypernetwork decomposition), but the project's own credibility standard
   — sealed worlds, frozen configs, then confirmation — is not yet met,
   and the remaining gap is roughly the SPEC_AUDIT execution order plus
   the confirmation run itself.
2. The preregistration story is only tellable once. Quoting development
   numbers publicly now spends it for asterisked claims that are days of
   compute from being asterisk-free.
3. One exception is allowed at any time: releasing the benchmark and
   methodology alone, with no results claims. That strengthens the
   preregistration story instead of spending it.

Action items this adds to the queue (beyond SPEC_AUDIT's own list):

- Commit `notes/` and `reviews/` — they are untracked, and an untracked
  original has already been lost to an overwrite once today.
- Re-derive the worlds 0-2 robustness means from run directories before
  any public text cites them (currently sourced only from
  reviewer-feedback-07; flagged in V2 spec Appendix A).
- Run the two free analyses (measured-recurrence crossover, rho*(N)
  truncation) BEFORE freezing summaries — they may upgrade the headline
  and cost nothing.
- Clean-checkout install/test/smoke rehearsal on the way to the
  confirmation gate, per EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md.

Everything with numbers waits for seeds 100-129. Report the confirmation
outcome whatever it is — a confirmed effect, an attenuated one, or a null
— under the reporting rules in RELEASE_PLAN.md.
