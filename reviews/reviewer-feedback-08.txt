# Reviewer feedback 08 — pre-confirmation assessment

Scope: review of progress through commit 79087f0 ("record exploratory
robustness results"): ten-world rho sweep, ten-world checkpoint replication,
hypernetwork control (stage-1 tuned), dense-24 control, retention refresh,
fingerprint validation, and the first robustness block (reverse order,
replay 0/1/4 on worlds 0-2). Verdict up front: V1 is close to done as a
trustworthy result. What remains is a short, closed list — and the
discipline to stop adding to it.

---

## 1. The robustness result is the strongest kind: boring

Dense-minus-Continuous advantage: reverse order +3,175; no replay +3,165;
canonical +3,492; heavy replay +3,082. Flat to within ~10% across every
condition, 3/3 worlds each. Two nuisance explanations die here pending the
worlds 3-9 extension:

- Not curriculum: reversing the order moves the effect by ~300 nats on a
  ~3,400 baseline.
- Not replay: the reusable substrate wins WITHOUT episodic rehearsal. This
  is the most important cell in the table — shared parameters alone carry
  prior-task information forward, which is the cleanest available version
  of "experience consolidated into a reusable computational substrate."
  Reviewer-07's reading is endorsed, including the refusal to build a
  replay-interaction story out of a flat 3,165/3,492/3,082 pattern.

Recommendation: when worlds 3-9 confirm, close both axes permanently. No
replay=2, no extra orderings, no fifth permutation. Marginal value ~zero.

## 2. The checkpoint replication contains a better result than the one
##    being quoted

Inspecting artifacts/checkpoints_development/sweep.json: at 8 tasks
completed, Dense is EQUAL OR SLIGHTLY BETTER at 32-shot novel adaptation
(world 0: 0.0276 vs 0.0306; world 1: 0.0155 vs 0.0158). The gap then opens
progressively; by 64 tasks Continuous is ~2x better on the worlds checked.

This is a cleaner learning-to-learn story than any gain ratio: the two
architectures START FROM THE SAME PLACE and DIVERGE WITH EXPERIENCE. The
figure to make: per-checkpoint paired difference (Dense minus Continuous
32-shot novel NMSE) vs tasks completed, mean and per-world traces across
all ten development worlds, showing a crossing near zero at checkpoint 8.

Consequences:
- Stop quoting "6.4x vs 2.3x" — it both overstates (ratio instability,
  feedback-05 s1) and understates (misses the equal-start divergence
  shape) the replicated result.
- The equal start at checkpoint 8 is itself a finding: the reusable
  advantage in novel adaptation is ACQUIRED, not architectural at
  initialization. That sentence belongs in the abstract.

## 3. The remaining list before the sealed worlds (finite; keep it so)

In priority order:

1. Hypernetwork control, final verdict. This is the last experiment that
   can change the INTERPRETATION rather than the confidence. If tuned
   Hypernetwork matches Continuous, the claim becomes "low-dimensional
   reusable operator structure" rather than "explicit basis slots" — a
   different and arguably more general paper. Stage-1 tuning is recorded;
   finish stage 2 under the same symmetric rule and report it either way.
   Note the retention angle from reviewer-07: if lifetime losses match,
   the 29,248-vs-33,928-bit gap becomes the interesting number (explicit
   basis = shorter description of the same function family).
2. Second model initialization across worlds 0-9 (one initialization is
   the only untested randomness axis).
3. The two free analyses from feedback-05, still outstanding and still
   free: (a) per-world crossover re-interpolated in measured functional
   recurrence coordinates; (b) rho*(N) from truncated-lifetime readings of
   existing prequential logs. (b) remains the highest-information-per-FLOP
   item in the project: it separates "amortization threshold" from "static
   representational bias" with zero new training, and its figure — the
   crossover moving with lifetime length — would be the strongest in the
   paper if it holds.
4. GELU crossover-shift test (feedback-05 s3, six runs) — folds the
   family-prior result and the crossover into one economic framework if
   the crossover shifts right rather than vanishing.
5. Robustness worlds 3-9 completion (already planned; nothing new).

Then freeze everything and open seeds 100-129. Nothing else joins this
list. Reviewer-07's closing warning is endorsed and extended: the notes
directory is generating ideas faster than any lifetime of compute can test
them, and that discipline applies to reviewers too.

## 4. Endorsements, briefly

- Fingerprint validation (reviewer-06's suggestion, implemented same day)
  closes the width-128 class of reproducibility bug properly.
- Retention is closed: int8 degradation at 1e-6 mean / 1.4e-4 worst is a
  solved question; stop spending on it. The retained-bits ordering
  (Discrete 26,208 < Continuous 29,248 < Hypernetwork 33,928 < Dense-24
  56,448 < Dense-C 66,688) diverging from the lifetime-loss ordering is
  the resource-frontier result, and it justifies the weighted objective
  J = L_preq + lambda*D + mu*C as measurement, not decoration.
- Shared-parent + residual with the Delta-vs-rho prediction
  (rho up => ||Delta|| down) is the right FIRST experiment of the next
  phase — the first model where the amount of sharing is learned — and it
  belongs after confirmation, not before. Guard against the residual
  escape hatch exactly as reviewer-07 specifies (tight rank/penalty, or
  it silently rebuilds Dense-C).
- The reverse-order analysis suggestion in reviewer-07 (compare the SLOPE
  of per-task cost vs lifetime position under both orderings, not just
  totals) should be folded into the worlds 3-9 robustness report while
  those runs are being done anyway.

## 5. What the paper now is

The supportable claim set, if the remaining list closes cleanly:

1. A benchmark (ROW) with validated ground truth, a passing oracle, and a
   sealed confirmation protocol.
2. A replicated crossover in preferred representation as latent functional
   recurrence increases — invariant to task order, replay budget,
   alpha-leakage, teacher rank, task-code size, and quantization — with
   honest boundaries where the family prior matters (GELU).
3. Progressive divergence in novel-composition learning: equal at 8 tasks,
   ~2x apart at 64 — learning-to-learn acquired over the lifetime.
4. A measured resource frontier (online learning vs storage vs inference)
   across dense, continuous, discrete, and hypernetwork representations,
   with real quantized retention numbers.
5. One falsified secondary hypothesis, reported as such.

That is a complete, honest, small-compute empirical paper about the
economics of abstraction. The confirmation run decides the error bars, not
the story. Finish the list, freeze, open the sealed worlds.
