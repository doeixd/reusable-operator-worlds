Reviewer feedback 71 — received 2026-08-23, on the H50 draft plan.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the right next experiment, and the sanity anchors are exactly the
ones I would want. Keep the overall design; tighten the scoring economy
before freezing — the current version spends compute on cells unlikely to
change the conclusion.

The most important anchor is m = 64 against the from-scratch L_4
endpoint: recovery fraction = (migration-induced separation recovered) /
(separation achieved by organized-from-scratch L_4), for both the C_LOO
margin and substitutability. It distinguishes cheap reorganization vs
partial migration vs essentially-retraining-required, which matters more
than whether TRUE eventually beats RANDOM. Make recovery fraction a
primary descriptive quantity even if not verdict-determining.

THE CURVE IS SCIENTIFICALLY IMPORTANT. S_TRUE(m) - S_WRONG(m) as a
function of reorganization effort tests a substantive hypothesis: is
there a modest m* with separation well before from-scratch retraining?
If separation appears quickly, a plausible sleep operator exists; if only
at m = 64 with near-total L_4 recovery, "sleep reorganization" may be
disguised retraining. Preserve a low/intermediate/high curve.

REDUCE SCORING COST BY SCORING FEWER m VALUES, NOT FEWER LOO TASKS. The
LOO statistic is what distinguishes TRUE from wrong partitions; halving
its sample weakens the core causal comparison, while dropping one scored
budget sacrifices little. Order: (1) keep all 64 LOO tasks; (2) score
m in {0, 16, 64}, scoring m = 4 only if the transition is already below
16; (3) sample tasks only if compute still demands it. Substitutability
is mechanistic corroboration, not the primary signal: score it only for
TRUE, the best WRONG/RANDOM, and SHAM, at m = 16 and 64.

THE MOST IMPORTANT CONTROL IS SHAM. The comparison that matters is
Delta_structure(h, m) = S(h, m) - S(SHAM, m); otherwise another 4,096
updates can make almost anything look better. Same start, optimizer,
data, trainable parameter count, and updates for every arm. If TRUE
improves but SHAM improves by the same amount, there is no abstraction
result.

WRONG STRUCTURES MUST BE MORE THAN ONE FLAVOUR, and after the H49 bug be
especially conservative: any discoverability verdict requires
TRUE > max(WRONG/RANDOM) by a preregistered margin — not TRUE > SHAM.
Apply the new constitutional rule literally.

RECORD THE MIGRATION COST ITSELF: extra steps, temporary state, the
functional drift during migration, and old-task loss immediately after
migration. The eventual decision is J_sleep(h) = C_migrate(h) +
lambda D*(R_h) + eta C_LOO(R_h); collect the ingredients now even if the
verdict does not use them. A TRUE organization that becomes better only
after 4,096 expensive steps may not be economically worthwhile.

PREDICTIONS. At m = 0, TRUE ~ WRONG (known). At m = 16, TRUE begins to
separate. At m = 64, clear TRUE advantage but NOT full recovery of L_4:
C_LOO recovery perhaps 25-50% at m = 16 and 50-80% at m = 64;
substitutability may recover somewhat faster. The most exciting result:
TRUE separates from every wrong partition at a modest budget while
recovering only part of the fully supervised endpoint — you do not need
to recreate the whole representation to know which structural hypothesis
is promising, which is exactly what a practical search operator needs.

INTERPRETATION DISCIPLINE. Separation by m = 16: strong evidence for
counterfactual reorganization as a discovery mechanism. Separation only
at m = 64 with near-100% recovery: closer to "retrain under each
hypothesis and select", valid but expensive. All candidates improving
similarly: the operator is extra optimization. A random partition
beating TRUE would be particularly informative — an economically
equivalent alternative ontology, or a retrospective objective still
misaligned with fertility; score the winning partition on held-out
siblings only AFTER selection, diagnostically.

THE CONNECTION IS DIRECT: this is the first experiment testing
abstraction discovery as counterfactual model restructuring rather than
probing or clustering. If it succeeds the loop is WAKE -> PROPOSE ->
REORGANIZE -> CROSS-VALIDATE on past experience -> KEEP/REJECT, and the
program-synthesis analogue (solved programs -> propose primitive ->
rewrite -> measure savings -> keep) is almost identical. Freeze after
tightening compute around fewer scored budgets, with TRUE-versus-best-
wrong load-bearing everywhere. The core question: can a useful
abstraction become visible after a cheap partial reorganization, rather
than requiring full retraining under the hypothesis? If yes, it is the
first plausible mechanism for autonomous sleep-time abstraction search.
