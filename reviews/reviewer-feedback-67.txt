Reviewer feedback 67 — received 2026-08-23, on the H47 B1 result (MIXED)
and the B2 generator extension.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

B1 is a useful negative/mixed result: the effect of discretizing a
genuinely continuous family is SMALLER than expected, and it localizes
where the cost appears. Cleanest summary: continuous representation
matters, but late discretization is surprisingly cheap. That is
encouraging for the larger program. The frozen verdict must remain MIXED,
but the directional structure is unusually coherent — M < H_late <
H_early < L_arb on both present cost and R_alpha in every development
world; the effect size was not large enough relative to the
preregistered one-SD bands to license that ordering.

WHAT B1 SUGGESTS. First, forcing an arbitrary ontology is meaningfully
costly: L_arb throws away 36-69% of M's present-task advantage, the one
effect large enough to clear the gate. A bad discrete vocabulary can
interfere with learning even when it remains expressively adequate; eps
repairs much of the mistake for the future, but the lifetime is less
efficient. Second, the cost of hardening itself is small: dlog R_alpha ~
0.04 late versus ~0.11 early. The picture is less "continuous good,
discrete bad" and more: continuous formation first, discrete compilation
later MAY be nearly lossless — closer to the original wake/sleep
hypothesis than expected. The key word remains "may": B1's table did not
license it, but it is the right developmental hypothesis to test again
where discreteness is actually justified.

THE INNOVATION CHANNEL AGAIN. The wrong partition badly hurts J_present
while barely-to-moderately hurting R_full: eps functions as a robustness
mechanism — structured abstraction can be imperfect because private
innovation repairs local representational errors — but cannot
retroactively recover the learning efficiency lost while the wrong
abstraction was in use. Three-channel roles: S persistent regularity;
alpha cheap structured variation; eps error correction / genuinely novel
computation. For synthesis later: a synthesizer need not get every
abstraction exactly right if it has a bounded local patch mechanism.

B2 IS EXACTLY THE RIGHT EXPERIMENT. Do not push B1 further on this
generator; there is no true membership variable. B2 creates T =
P_g(alpha) + eps with g a discrete group and alpha continuous within-
group variation — the first world where the learner must infer operator
identity versus operator arguments, a precursor to program induction.

MAKE THE B2 PRE-RUN GATE UNUSUALLY STRICT. Before any M/L/H lifetime,
establish from the generator: (1) exact backward compatibility, G = 1
bit-for-bit, no approximate compatibility; (2) within-group continuity —
a held-out family should be strongly representable by its group's shared
coordinates, otherwise you have clusters without useful internal
parameterization; (3) cross-group non-substitutability — a schema from
group g1 should fit held-out tasks of g2 materially worse than its own,
Q = L(P_g' -> T_g) / L(P_g -> T_g) substantially above 1, else membership
is nominal again; (4) membership must have ECONOMIC value — an oracle
route should materially improve at least the structured interface,
R_alpha,L_true < R_alpha,unstructured - delta, on cheap development
worlds before launching the full comparison. Existence before discovery.

ONE DESIGN ISSUE TO WATCH. Completely disjoint rank-2 subspaces may make
membership too easy, reducing B2 to obvious clustering. Eventually a
separation knob rho_group interpolating from clearly distinct schemas
through ambiguous to the G = 1 continuous world would test whether
discrete identity becomes worthwhile exactly when functional separation
is large enough — connecting back to the amortization laws: when does it
become cheaper to name two manifolds than to stretch one? Do NOT add that
complexity to the first B2 test; get one clean positive membership world
first.

PREDICTIONS FOR B2. Effects larger than B1. R_alpha,M > R_alpha,L_true by
a measurable amount, but R_full,M ~ R_full,L_true: membership discovery
costs in the structured channel while innovation buffers the full system.
For hardening, more strongly than B1: H_early < H_late <~ M, because
committing to the wrong discrete group now has semantic consequences. If
late hardening is still near-neutral, that is a substantially stronger
result than B1: learn continuously while uncertain; compile into discrete
identities once evidence accumulates — a real wake/sleep result because
the world now actually contains discrete identities.

FOR THE LANGUAGE: B1 taught us not to manufacture discrete primitives
when the world gives one continuous parameterized family; B2 asks when
the opposite is true. The hierarchy is P_g(alpha), not P(alpha) for
everything nor P_1, P_2, P_3 for every variant; composition is
P_g3(alpha_3) o P_g2(alpha_2) o P_g1(alpha_1) and synthesis infers the
(g, alpha) pairs — a neural analogue of operator + arguments before
program structure is introduced. Principle: continuous parameterization
first; discrete identities only where the environment makes them real
and useful. Proceed to the schema_groups generator and its pre-lifetime
separation/opportunity audits; if those gates pass, freeze B2.
