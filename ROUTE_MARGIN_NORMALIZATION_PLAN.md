# Route-margin normalization: does the geometry result survive comparability? (Tier 0)

Status: written before any number exists, committed with its code and tests.
Tier 0, descriptive: frozen stage-2 models and committed reports only, no
training, no new world, NO verdict. It cannot change `SO2_FAILS`, `SO3_FAILS`,
`SO4_FAILS`, the world-quality census's `MIXED`, or the geometry census's
`GEOMETRY-EXPLAINS`.

# Question

The library-geometry census (`62301db`) returned GEOMETRY-EXPLAINS on the
strength of ONE measure, `route_margin`, with four disclosed weaknesses:

- its registered world-8 clause passed by 2.6% on a single cell;
- it spans 0.23-67.48 and the plan itself states it is not comparable across
  task sets;
- per run it is -0.50 (SO2, 3 cells), **+0.12 (SO3, wrong sign)**, -0.64 (SO4);
- the census's own primary measure (`min_pair`, slot duplication) carried nothing.

So the effect may be real, or it may be an artifact of pooling a
scale-incomparable quantity across three task sets. This census decides that
with no new compute beyond minutes, and it is the step that must happen before
any world band is spent on a prefix or geometry screen.

# Two registered tests, both on the 24 cells already in hand

## Test A - within-world concordance (removes the scale problem by construction)

Seven worlds have three replay streams each: SO3 w3, w4, w5 and SO4 w6, w7, w8,
w9. SO2's three cells have one stream each and are EXCLUDED from Test A, which is
disclosed rather than worked around.

For each of those 7 worlds: does the stream with the WORST stage-3 terminal error
hold the LOWEST `route_margin` of its three streams?

- Statistic: `k` = the number of worlds where it does, out of 7.
- Null: under no relation, the worst-outcome stream is equally likely to hold any
  of the three margins, so `k ~ Binomial(7, 1/3)`; expected 2.33. The exact
  upper-tail probability of the observed `k` is reported.
- This never compares a margin across worlds, so the 300x scale spread and the
  differing task sets cannot produce it.

## Test B - a library-intrinsic margin (comparable across worlds by construction)

`route_margin` mixes the library with its teacher task set. Test B replaces the
task set with the library's OWN routes, so the measure is a property of the
library alone:

For each stage-2 library, with `K = 16` routes drawn by
`SeedSequence([2037, world, stream])` over its 12 slots and 2 steps, and a fixed
64-state probe (`SeedSequence([2031, world, stream])`, matching the geometry
census's probe seed):

1. generate `support_y = library.hard(support_x, route)`, so the generating route
   is by construction the exact minimizer;
2. compute every route's support MSE with `all_route_support_mse` (144 routes);
3. `self_margin` for that route = the SECOND-lowest support MSE divided by the
   variance of `support_y`, a scale-free measure of how wrong the best
   alternative route is;
4. the library's `self_margin` is the median over the 16 routes.

High `self_margin` means the library's routes are intrinsically identifiable.
Because the targets are generated by the library itself, the quantity carries no
teacher information and is directly comparable across worlds.

- Statistic: pooled Spearman of `self_margin` with stage-3 terminal error over all
  24 cells, with the one-sided permutation null used by the previous two censuses
  (2000 permutations, `SeedSequence([2029])`), plus per-run values and the
  fail/pass median separation.

## Reported beside (no thresholds)

- Within-world z-scored `route_margin`, pooled Spearman over the 21 three-stream
  cells.
- The same concordance count for stage-2 terminal ERROR, so Test A's result can be
  compared against the world-quality census's predictor on identical footing.
- `self_margin` against `route_margin` (does the intrinsic measure track the
  task-set one at all?).

# Guards (the census refuses to report unless all pass)

- 24 cells; every library hash equals the hash its own run recorded.
- **Non-vacuity for Test B:** the generating route must be recovered as the
  argmin for at least 15 of 16 routes in EVERY library. If a library fails this,
  `self_margin` is not measuring identifiability there and the census stops.
- `self_margin` finite, positive, and not constant across cells.
- Test A uses exactly 7 worlds and 21 cells; each world contributes exactly one
  concordance decision, with ties in `route_margin` (none expected) reported
  rather than broken.
- Tie-corrected ranks and nan on zero variance (2026-09-16 learning).

# Triage (decides only what the PI is asked next)

- **SURVIVES:** `k >= 5` of 7 (exact Binomial(7, 1/3) upper tail 0.045) OR
  `self_margin` reaches `|rho| >= 0.5` with permutation `<= 0.05`. Then route
  identifiability is a real correlate of stage-3 success, measured without the
  scale artifact, and a registered screen is worth planning - with its own world
  band, which the PI must approve.
- **DISSOLVES:** `k <= 3` AND `self_margin` `|rho| < 0.3`. Then the geometry
  census's effect was an artifact of pooling incomparable magnitudes. Report it,
  withdraw the geometry reading as a basis for any intervention, and close the
  prefix/geometry screen line. The online result then stands as world-dependent
  with no identified mechanism, which is what the paper says.
- **MIXED:** anything else; report and take no successor decision from it.

# Disclosed confounds and limits

- 21 cells in Test A, 24 in Test B; three seeds; one protocol; observational.
- Test A's null assumes the three streams are exchangeable within a world, which
  is true of the replay seed by construction but not of anything else that
  differs between them.
- `self_margin` is measured on self-generated targets, so it says how
  identifiable the library's routes are to a perfect learner, NOT how identifiable
  the world's tasks are.
- Stage 2 only; stage 3 continues to change the library.
- A surviving correlation still does not show that improving identifiability
  would improve stage 3.

# Cost

Minutes: 24 small libraries, 16 routes each, 144-route support-MSE passes on a
64-state probe, plus one permutation null. Single process, no lifetime.
